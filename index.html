One address, three things

A kitchen, a shop and a room worth booking.

Pipoca is run by a small Brazilian team on Brixton Road. The cooking pulls from France, Brazil and the Caribbean, and none of it uses animal products. Vegans come for the range. Everyone else comes back for the feijoada.

01

The kitchen

Savoury and sweet crêpes, buckwheat galettes, all-day brunch plates and Brazilian bowls, cooked to order.

02

The shop

A refill store for organic dry goods, cleaning and body products, with nut butter ground in front of you.

03

The space

A calm room under a ceiling of dried flowers, available for supper clubs, workshops and private dinners.
